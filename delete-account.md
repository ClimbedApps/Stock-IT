# Delete your Stock-IT account

You only have a Stock-IT account if you signed in with Google to use sharing, sync and backup. Without one, everything is on your phone and uninstalling the app removes it.

## In the app

1. Open Stock-IT and tap **Settings** in the bar at the bottom.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

This removes you from your household and deletes your sign-in. Your places, items and lists stay on your phone. If you own a household, remove everyone else from it first (on the Household tab).

## By email

If you no longer have the app, email [ClimbedApps@gmail.com](mailto:climbedapps@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Stock-IT%20account) from the Google account you signed in with. Your account and your membership of any household are deleted within 30 days.

## What's deleted

- Your sign-in: name, email address and user ID.
- Your household membership and your entries in the household's activity feed.
- If you own the household and no one else is in it, the household and everything in it.

Stock and lists that other members added stay with them.

Even if you don't delete your account, a sign-in that isn't in any household and hasn't been used for 90 days is deleted automatically.
